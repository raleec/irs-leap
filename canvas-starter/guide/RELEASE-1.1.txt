Source state: Draft0/status1, Flow service Stopped.
Trigger: Added only, LEAP Request, organization scope, Pending filter.
Operation: ONE native Approve/Reject First-to-respond approval, P7D timeout,
notifications enabled in the definition BUT flow stopped, reassignment disabled.
Original selected recipients are resolved from stored Users lookups; response

precede protected final writes. Unexpected output is not a denial.

status and app link. No medical description/category/date in mail templates.
